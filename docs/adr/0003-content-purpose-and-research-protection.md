# Place content by purpose and protect approved research

Independently useful concepts, principles, and methods belong in `40_Knowledge`. Results, conditions, evidence, and analysis tied to specific papers, experiments, or research questions belong in `50_Research`. A paper origin does not determine the destination.

AI-generated research uses Draft → Review → ChangeSet, and later AI changes require human approval. This preserves Knowledge and Research purposes without introducing a research write path that bypasses review. Folder placement alone does not establish approval.
